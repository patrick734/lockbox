Is it locked?

Paste a token or LP token address to see how much of its supply is locked in Lockbox, and when each lock opens. Locked tokens can't be withdrawn early by anyone, including us.

Lock tokens

Lock your project's tokens or liquidity so holders can see it can't be pulled. You keep the right to withdraw once the lock opens; until then, nobody can move them.

Locking costs a flat fee in BOT plus gas. You can push the unlock date later, but never earlier.

My locks

Withdraw what has opened, push an unlock date later, or hand a lock to another wallet.
